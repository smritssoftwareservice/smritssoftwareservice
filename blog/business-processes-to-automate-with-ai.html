10 Business Processes You Can Automate With AI

Published by Smrits Software Service Engineering Team

Organizations worldwide are turning to custom AI automation to reduce operational drag and scale revenue. Here are 10 key business processes you can automate today using modern AI systems built by Smrits Software Service:

  1. Inbound Lead Qualification & Routing: Automatically score lead intent and route high-value buyers to dedicated account reps.
  2. Customer Support Ticket Resolution: Use AI conversational agents to instantly resolve routine customer inquiries over Web, Email, or WhatsApp.
  3. Document & Invoice Data Extraction: Extract key fields from receipts, contracts, and PDFs with zero manual keying errors.
  4. Content & Social Media Post Generation: Auto-generate brand-aligned blog drafts, marketing copy, and multi-channel posts.
  5. Real Estate Lead Follow-Up: Send instant personalized property recommendations and schedule site visits (see EstateAI).
  6. CRM Contact Data Sync: Automatically enrich contact records and summarize interaction histories after calls or email exchanges.
  7. Email Response Drafting: Generate contextual response drafts for support representatives to review and send in one click.
  8. Inventory & Demand Forecasting: Predict stock requirements based on historical buying trends and seasonal metrics.
  9. Visual Quality Inspection: Detect manufacturing defects on assembly lines using automated computer vision models.
  10. Internal Knowledge Management: Implement custom AI semantic search engines over internal company wikis and PDF archives.
